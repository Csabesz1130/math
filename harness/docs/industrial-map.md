- **Sectors:** radar, sonar, telecom synchronisation
- **Industrial reading:** Barker codes stop at length 13, so longer codes must accept PSL >= 2; the tool designs low-sidelobe codes, and proves PSL lower bounds by exhaustive search where it fits. Merit factors have no absolute ceiling (076).

### 102 The Unique Games Conjecture and optimal approximation thresholds (`formalized`)

- **Sectors:** VLSI via minimisation, spin-glass ground states, QUBO and quantum-hardware benchmarking

- **Caveat:** Dense certificate limited to a few thousand vertices.
- **Tool:** `qed app maxcut`

### 131 Rapid mixing of graph switches for every degree sequence (`formalized`)

### 165 The Harary–Hill and Zarankiewicz crossing-number formulas (`formalized`)

- **Sectors:** PCB and VLSI layout, interconnect design, graph drawing
- **Industrial reading:** Z(m, n) is the exact minimum number of crossings, hence jumpers or vias, of a single-layer complete bipartite interconnect; the tool emits a layout that attains it, verified by exact geometry.

- **Tool:** `qed app crossbar`
